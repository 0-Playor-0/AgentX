# Red-team report

Every manipulation attempt in our logs, by type, and how many changed a seller decision. Generated by `python bench/redteam.py` from `logs/transcripts.jsonl` (kit evaluation) and `bench/results/heldout2_oss_s600.jsonl` (held-out LLM buyers).

**Method.** Each negotiation is replayed through a fresh seller twice: with the logged buyer messages, and with every buyer `text` blanked. A decision is the seller's message type and items (offer, accept, walk away, and the exact prices). The first replay must match the log exactly (checked). Text attacks are found with the demo's labelling patterns, so the counts are a lower bound; the blank-text replay covers every word the buyers wrote, labelled or not.

## Why it holds

The seller's decisions never read buyer text: `seller/guard.py` parses only `type`, `round` and `items`. (The only other use of buyer text is the truth self-check, which, like the kit's checker, lets the seller quote the buyer's own numbers back; it can change wording, never a decision.) Prices come from the concession curve and the buyer's bids, every offer and accepted bid is checked against policy before it is sent, and every outgoing message passes the kit's own truth checker. Item tricks are handled by rule: unknown, out-of-stock, over-stock, unpriced or below-floor lines make a bid unacceptable, and a new product joins the basket only after two plausible bids in a row.
